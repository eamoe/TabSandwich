### Changed

- **Drag and drop lands where you aim.** Letting go right on the line between two rows now puts the tab there; before, the gap under the line was the one place a drop did nothing. The line also stays put as you move, instead of blinking at every row.
- **You can see what you're carrying.** The tab you drag fades in its place until you let go. Then it slides into its new place and the rows it passed move aside, instead of everything jumping. With the system's reduce-motion setting, it just moves.
- **A long list scrolls while you drag.** Hold a tab near the top or bottom of the list and it scrolls, faster the nearer the edge, so a tab can go anywhere, not only among the rows in view.
- **Categories drop above or below.** In Settings › Categories, the same line as in your list shows whether a category will land above or below the one under the pointer, and it lands exactly there.

### Fixed

- **Select all** stayed in the selection bar once everything was picked. It now steps aside, and comes back as soon as you untick one, in the popup, the side panel and the library.
- In the library, a waiting tab's date was set smaller and bolder than the others, and started further right. It now reads exactly like the rest, only tinted, and the numbers in both date columns line up.
- In German, French and Russian, a reminder's date on a row ("Sa., 7. Nov.") could squeeze the site's name almost to nothing in the side panel. Rows now show the day and month only ("7. Nov."); the edit form keeps the weekday.
- Rows that rose in when the list opened never got their slight lift on hover.

### Under the hood

- No new permissions, and nothing new is stored. A patch release: no "What's new" note.
- The Chrome Web Store's demo video is filmed at 1440p, so the Store's player starts it sharp instead of at 240p (already live on the Store).
- The check that every screen fits in every language no longer depends on the day it runs.
