### New

- **Tab Sandwich speaks your language.** It follows Chrome's own language: German, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, as well as English. Your own category names, titles and notes are never translated.
- **Tell us when a word is off.** The translations are new, so Settings › About now asks: spotted a clumsy word, or have an idea? **Tell us** opens a short form with your language already filled in. Write in any language.
- **A new install starts in its language.** Its four starter categories and the random names of saved windows ("Toasted Rye") come in that language too. From then on they're yours, and switching languages never renames them.

### Improved

- **Everything fits, in every language.** Long words no longer cut off the search box's hint, **Move to…** or the selection bar's buttons.
- **Roomier forms and save card.** In the add and edit forms the category has a row of its own, so its name shows whole, and on a page you've already saved, **Show** (or **Restore**) is a link beside "Saved 12 days ago", leaving the row to the category and **Update**.
- **The library in a narrower window.** Titles keep room to read: the Opened column, then Site, step aside until the window is wide again, and dates like "300 days ago" fit their column.

### Under the hood

- Every screen is checked in every language for words that don't fit, with three-digit counts.
- Every count reads naturally in each language ("1 tab", "2 tabs"; Russian has three forms), and dates and alphabetical order follow the language too.
- The Chrome Web Store page is in every language too: its description, rewritten around what you come to do, and its screenshots, each showing the app in that language. A 30-second demo video shows it at work.
