### New

- **Tab Sandwich speaks your language.** It follows Chrome's own language: German, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, as well as English. Your own category names, titles and notes are never translated.

### Under the hood

- Every count reads naturally in each language ("1 tab", "2 tabs"; Russian has three forms), and dates and alphabetical order follow the language too.
