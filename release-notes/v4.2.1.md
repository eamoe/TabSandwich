### Changed

- **Saving a window says when its pages are in your archive.** If every page open in the window is already saved and some are in your archive, "Save all tabs in this window" no longer just disappears: the line says "2 open tabs are in your archive" and offers **Restore**, which brings them back to your list (as the saved window they were, if they were one), with Undo. After saving a window, the note now counts the pages it skipped because they're in your archive apart from those already in your list.

### Fixed

- **Keyboard focus is easier to see on a saved tab.** The row you're on now gets a full accent ring drawn inside its edge. Before, the first row's ring was cut thinner along its top, where the list's scrolling edge clipped it.
- **A saved window's ring goes round its whole row,** ⋯ button included, the same as a tab's. It used to stop short of the ⋯.
- **↓ from the search box reaches a saved window at the top of your list.** It used to skip past it, to the first tab below (or, with the window open, to its own first tab).
- **The library's column headers show their whole ring.** Tabbing through Title, Category, Site, Saved and Opened, the ring showed only on a header's left and bottom; it's now a rounded ring inside the header, on all four sides.

### Under the hood

- No new permissions, and nothing new is stored. A patch release: no "What's new" note.
- New approved screenshots of each focus ring and of the archived window's line, in light and dark; the new tests were each seen failing on the old code first. The every-language check now also fails if the archived window's line wraps onto a second line.
